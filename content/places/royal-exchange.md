Sir Thomas Gresham, the merchant Gresham Street is named after, founded it in 1565 as London's place for trading goods. The building you see is the third on the site. The first was lost in the Great Fire of 1666 and the second burned down in 1838. Today it holds shops and restaurants around a covered courtyard.
