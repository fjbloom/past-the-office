Designed by Christopher Wren and Robert Hooke to mark the Great Fire of 1666. At 202 feet tall, it stands 202 feet from the baker's shop in Pudding Lane where the fire is said to have begun. A spiral stair of 311 steps leads to the viewing gallery.
